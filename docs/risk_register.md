# Shadow Voice — Risk Register (v2)

This register documents all technical, regulatory, carrier, and operational risks identified across Phases 0 through 5. Every risk is assigned an owner, severity level, mitigation control, and validation criteria.

---

| ID | Category | Risk Description | Severity | Owner | Mitigation Control & Verification | Status |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---|:---:|
| **R-01** | **Legal / Consent** | Risk of falling under TCPA artificial/prerecorded voice restrictions without proper consent. | **CRITICAL** | External Legal / PM | Identify legal basis per campaign/number class before dialing; require Prior Express Written Consent (PEWC) for wireless/residential; external legal counsel review before Phase 4 sign-off. | **Active / Controlled** |
| **R-02** | **Legal / TCPA** | Abandoned-call safe harbor assumes a live sales rep connection; product is AI-driven. | **CRITICAL** | External Legal / PM | Resolve whether AI qualifies as live rep before live predictive dialing; gate Phase 4 audit on written counsel confirmation. | **Pending Counsel Sign-off** |
| **R-03** | **Compliance** | National DNC registry staleness exceeding 31 days. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Automated DNC engine (`dnc.py`) blocks all outbound dialing if snapshot age > 31.0 days (`is_national_dnc_fresh() == False`). | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-04** | **Compliance** | Internal DNC suppression request failing to propagate immediately across all nodes. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Centralized real-time internal DNC list with zero-latency pre-dial lookup and VICIdial read-back verification. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-05** | **Compliance** | Calling window violation caused by wrong called-party timezone. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Timezone resolution engine (`call_window.py`) maps NPA area codes to IANA zones, enforcing 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM local time restriction. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-06** | **Compliance** | Predictive dialing causing regulatory abandonment rate to exceed 3.0% FCC cap. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Separated metrics: technical disconnects tracked separately from regulatory abandonments; automated warning at 2.5% and kill-switch trigger at 3.0%. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-07** | **Performance** | Multi-session TTS concurrency degrading latency under 5-line load. | **HIGH** | Conversational AI Eng | Benchmark 1 to 5 concurrent sessions under sustained load; measure P50/P95/P99 latency distribution to verify P95 < 650 ms. | **Verified (<540ms)** |
| **R-08** | **Performance** | Latency drift hiding behind aggregate averages. | **MEDIUM** | Conversational AI Eng | Strict percentile metrics (P50, P90, P95, P99) captured per turn and exported to Prometheus. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-09** | **Audio / DSP** | PSTN background noise causing VAD false triggers or missed turns. | **MEDIUM** | Conversational AI Eng | Adaptive noise floor tracking (`vad.py`) calibrated for 280–320 ms speech endpointing with PSTN noise injection tests. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-10** | **Audio / Buffer** | Bursty TTS causing audio buffer underrun/clipping over PSTN. | **HIGH** | Telephony Eng | Separate ingress jitter buffer (40-60ms) and paced egress playout buffer (monotonic clock 20ms cadence) with PLC silence injection. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-11** | **Architecture** | Shared session state leaking audio or conversational context between calls. | **CRITICAL** | Telephony Eng | Complete session isolation: dedicated `TelephonySession` instances, isolated jitter/playout buffers, UUID correlation, and no shared turn state. | **Verified Clean** |
| **R-12** | **Reliability** | Upstream provider outage (STT, LLM, or TTS) mid-call. | **HIGH** | Conversational AI Eng | Explicit timeouts, graceful fallback line, and circuit breaker (`outage_monitor.py`) that trips kill switch after 3 consecutive failures. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-13** | **Legal / IP** | OmniVoice-Studio or Qwen3-TTS fork license prohibiting commercial telephony use. | **MEDIUM** | Conversational AI Eng / PM | Direct Qwen3-TTS backend interface behind Shadow Voice abstract adapter; pinned commit/version and completed license audit. | **Audited & Pinned** |
| **R-14** | **Compliance** | Compliance logs lost or unreconstructable during TCPA audit/subpoena. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Immutable SQLite audit store (`audit_logger.py`) with SHA-256 cryptographic hash chaining, 5-year retention, and automated backup/restore verification. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-15** | **Legal / Privacy** | Wiretapping / call-recording consent violation in two-party consent states. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Pre-dial state lookup (`legal_gates.py`) identifies two-party consent states (CA, FL, IL, PA, etc.) and enforces required disclosures/settings. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-16** | **Carrier** | Calls blocked or labeled "Scam Likely" despite technical health. | **HIGH** | Telephony Eng / PM | STIR/SHAKEN A-attestation coordination, caller ID number ownership verification, and robocall mitigation database registration. | **Carrier Onboarding Tracked** |
| **R-17** | **Carrier / Timeline** | Slow carrier turnaround for STIR/SHAKEN and caller ID blowing 40-day delivery schedule. | **HIGH** | PM | Initiated carrier onboarding on Day 1 (Phase 0) with tracked ticket number; reviewed daily in async standups. | **Ticket Tracked** |
| **R-18** | **AI Safety / Drift** | LLM improvising unapproved pricing, promises, or commitments mid-call. | **CRITICAL** | Dialogue Eng / Conversational AI Eng | Dual-layer guardrail: prompt constraints plus real-time regex/semantic classification pass between LLM output and TTS input (`guardrail.py`); routes to human transfer on violation. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-19** | **Operations** | Cold start at full 5-line concurrency causing cascading failures. | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng / PM | Phase 4.5 Staged Canary Rollout: 1 line for full day (Day 36), scale to 3 lines (Day 37), then promote to 5 lines only if metrics clear. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-20** | **Operations** | Silent production degradation post-cutover (e.g. latency creep, DNC scrub job failure). | **HIGH** | QA/Compliance Eng | Phase 5 Ongoing Monitoring: Prometheus metrics exporter and rich terminal dashboard with real-time alerting on drop rates and DNC age. | **Operational** |
| **R-21** | **Data Governance** | Lead history store accumulating cross-call personal data without retention controls. | **MEDIUM** | Telephony Eng / QA | Lead history database (`lead_history.py`) governed under the identical 5-year compliance retention schedule and access controls as audit logs. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-22** | **Financial** | Runaway GPU or API costs exceeding budget. | **HIGH** | Telephony Eng / PM | Spend ceiling guard (`spend_guard.py`) enforces hard daily ($500) and campaign caps, triggering kill switch upon breach. | **Enforced & Tested** |
| **R-23** | **Business / Legal** | Third-party claims or telemarketing statutory litigation exposure. | **HIGH** | Founder (Faisal) | Business-side TCPA / E&O liability insurance sized to campaign volume and call destinations. | **Founder Tracked** |
